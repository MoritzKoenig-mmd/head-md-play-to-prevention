# Cyber Leak

You wet yourself on stage during the school play. Someone films it, and by the next day Matthew has turned a frame of you pissing yourself into a sticker with the caption this could be us but you playing. It’s spammed in the group chat and in private chats. People remix it with new captions, pasting your scared face onto pigeons, urinals and piles of shit, and every remix gets more laughs than the last. Now everyone at school knows your face. You don’t feel like going to school anymore. The voices and laughs are getting too loud. You stop showing up. Make it stop. You delete every account. Eventually you change schools. Months later, on your first day at the new school, a boy you’ve never met leans over to show you his favorite sticker. It’s an image you know too well: the sticker of your pissed pants.


Concepts: 
- Memes 
- Stickers 
- community rules 
- Conformity
- overton window

Everybody wants to be in on the joke. Every iteration has to be more extreme than the one before to "keep the joke alive"
