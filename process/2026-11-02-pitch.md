# Flip Side


You play the pinball machine from your youth and slowly start daydreaming, as a goblin inside the machine brings back the emotions you buried in middle school by bullying you while you play.


## Pitch

You start playing a pinball machine you played as a kid. The longer you play, the further you drift into a daydream, and the realistic machine takes on a weird, dreamlike look. A goblin inside it bullies you and represents the emotions you pushed away back then, which are now slowly coming back. As you advance through the levels and get closer to meeting the goblin, the memories get clearer, and you have to face what really happened: whether you were the one being bullied or the one doing the bullying.
