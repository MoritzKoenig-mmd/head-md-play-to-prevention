# Flip off!

Meet Flippy the pinball machine! 

![](./images/tilt.png)

## Pitch
Flip off! is a pinball game where the table has a personality, and that personality is a bully. Every level you clear makes the machine meaner: it traps your ball in bumper circles, covers the screen with spit and HAHAs, rewrites its own text to mock you, takes your flippers away, and resets the score just before you win. Then the final level pulls back the curtain and reveals that the machine is run by an angry, sad gnome, which gives the player a moment to stop and reflect on why people bully and what it does to the person on the other end. TILT is made for cyberbullying prevention: players feel the tactics of online harassment first, and recognize them afterwards.

## Bully mechanics
**The ball**
- Stuck in a circle of bumpers
- Things moved into your way, or away from where you need them
- Fake balls as a distraction
- The ball gets annoyingly slow
- The machine shakes itself to mess up your shot

**The flippers**
- Flippers stop working
- Flippers disappear
- Flipper controls change

**The screen**
- Spit blocks part of the screen
- Words block the screen
- HAHAs block your vision
- HAHAs push the ball away

**The text**
- Text hallucinations: the machine rewrites its own text
- "This is not the rainbow land, actually this is the hell of despair and anguish"

**The score**
- Resetting just before you win

## How it maps to cyberbullying
- Bumper circle: a pile-on you can't get out of
- Words and HAHAs on screen: mocking comments crowding your feed
- Text hallucinations: rumors rewriting what's true
- Flippers disappearing: being silenced
- Reset before winning: moving the goalposts
- Fake balls: distraction and gaslighting
