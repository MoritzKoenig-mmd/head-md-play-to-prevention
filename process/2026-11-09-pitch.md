# Without a Trace
- Adam HUDEC
- Moritz KÖNIG
- Sarah COURTADE

## Project
A story about the signs of cyberbullying, seen through the eyes of a younger sibling who is too young to understand them.

## Pitch
Every night, 8-year-old Coco sneaks out the Nintendo 3DS their parents took away from their older sibling Vivi. Hidden on the console are signs that Vivi is being bullied, but Coco doesn't understand them. The player does, but can only act as Coco, and can't leave a trace.

## Setup
2014, the family home, at night. Vivi (12) has just started middle school, apart from Coco for the first time. With no friends at the new school, Vivi is bullied by classmates through the 3DS and escapes into it, until their grades drop and the parents start hiding it every evening. Each night begins with finding the new hiding spot. The clues are in the games, the files and the decorations of the console.
