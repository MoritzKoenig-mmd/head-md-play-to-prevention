# Without a trace: Game Design Document
 
Sarah Courtade, Adam Hudec, Moritz König 
 
## Pitch
 
Every night, 8-year-old Coco secretly takes the Nintendo 3DS their parents confiscated from older sibling Vivi. Through Vivi's games, chats, doodles and stickers, Coco realises Vivi is being bullied and struggling with their identity. Coco has to choose: tell someone or stay silent.
 
|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Platform | Web, mobile first |
| Genre | Educational interactive story about cyberbullying |
| Length | 30 to 40 minutes: 6 or 8 nights of about 7 minutes |
| Audience | Students aged 12 to 16, in class or at home |
 
## Dilemma
 
Coco only knows because they invaded Vivi's privacy. Telling means admitting it and risking Vivi's trust. Staying silent lets things get worse. Like many young people who witness bullying, Coco fears betraying someone, being called a snitch, or getting into trouble.
 
## Learning objectives
 
- Spot the warning signs: withdrawal, slipping grades, secrecy, constant device use, anxiety about others seeing it
- Understand why victims stay silent, and that telling a trusted adult is not betrayal
- Know how to help: tell a trusted adult, save evidence, block and report, support the person being bullied
- Know where to get help
## Pillars
 
1. **The choice is the core.** The clues found decide whether Coco can tell.
2. **Loyalty has a price.** Telling exposes Coco's intrusion and cracks the parents' perfect-family image.
3. **The 3DS is the whole world.** The player sees only the console and links clues across apps. Digital stickers bring a physical object into the virtual.
4. **Silence protects no one.** Only action starts the healing.
## Characters
 
- **Coco (8):** player character, holds the choice
- **Vivi (12):** owns the 3DS, stays silent, vents anger in games
- **Parents:** guard the perfect-family image; punish Vivi when they catch them searching for the 3DS
- **Classmates:** laugh along and do nothing
## Core loop
 
Each night the player explores Vivi's 3DS until a hidden timer ends it. Vivi adds content by day, so the console changes every night.
 
- 2 of 4 clue spots hold a clue each night: stickers (ripped or replaced), Mii avatar (changes), game chat (keyboard suggestions), doodle gallery (new drawings)
- Missed clues don't return but turn into new ones
- No score shown; a comment from Coco and an audiovisual cue confirm a find
- 14 clues in total, 8 needed to unlock telling
## Endings
 
After the last night, the game checks the clues. With fewer than 8, telling is shown but greyed out.
 
- **Tell a teacher (good):** adults step in and Vivi learns about the intrusion. After some tension the siblings grow closer, and Vivi remakes their Mii.
- **Stay silent (bad):** Vivi runs away from home and leaves a note.
## Classroom use
 
Students play individually, then discuss in a teacher-led exercise supported by a worksheet.
